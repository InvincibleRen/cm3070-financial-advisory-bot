# S&P 500 Additions and Removals: Event Study

Generated at: 2026-09-24 13:54:34

## Setup

- Events: every membership change since 2015-01-01 in the point-in-time constituent file (504 changes; 1 add/remove pair(s) dropped as ticker changes: AVB->VMRK).
- Day 0 = first trading day on or after the effective date. A window `a..b` runs from the close of day a-1 to the close of day b, so `-5..-1` ends at the close when index funds trade.
- **Excess return** = stock return minus SPY return over the same days (market-adjusted). Prices are dividend-adjusted. A beta-adjusted version (beta from days -310..-61) is given at the end as a robustness check.
- **Beat SPY** = share of events whose excess return was positive (the "probability of rising relative to the market").
- **t (by date)**: several changes often share one effective date and one market move, so each date's events are averaged first and the t-stat is taken across dates. |t| above about 2 is the usual bar.
- Only effective dates are available. The announcement is usually about five trading days earlier, so windows ending at day -1 already contain the announcement reaction; a trader could only have acted after the announcement.

## Caveats

- Removals that stopped trading (acquisitions, bankruptcies) mostly have no yfinance data at all, so the removal groups under-represent the worst and the acquired outcomes.
- Many windows are tested across several groups; a single |t| slightly above 2 is weak evidence on its own.
- Descriptive research on past data, not financial advice.
