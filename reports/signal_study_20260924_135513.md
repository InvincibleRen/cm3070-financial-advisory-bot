# Do the Advisor's Technical Signals Work?

Generated at: 2026-09-24 13:55:13

## Setup

- Universe: point-in-time S&P 500 (each day's actual members)
- Stocks: 594 with prices out of 742 members since 2015-01-01
- Member stock-days: 1,258,477 (2015-10-16 to 2026-09-21)
- Rule: the advisor's score from price vs SMA20/50/200, golden/death crosses, MACD vs signal and the ADX amplifier (`backtester.calculate_backtest_signals`); Buy at score >= 3, Sell at <= -3. The live advisor scales the cross by its strength (+1 to +3) where this replay uses a fixed +2, and adds a small intraday nudge; otherwise the rules match.
- Each event is entered at that day's close and held 5, 20 or 60 trading days.
- **Beat same-day universe**: did the stock beat the average S&P 500 member over the same days? This removes the market's direction and is the cleanest test of whether the signal picks better stocks. A useful Buy signal should be well above 50% and its mean clearly positive; a useful Sell signal the opposite.
- t-stats: each day's events are averaged first, then a Newey-West t-stat with as many lags as the holding period (holding periods of consecutive days overlap). |t| above about 2 is the usual bar. The mean shown is pooled over events, while the t-stat tests the average *day*, so days with many events weigh less in the t-stat; when the effect is near zero the two can even differ in sign.
